# Variable Brightness LED Lamp

A custom LED lamp designed from schematic through PCB, firmware,
mechanical enclosure, and final assembly.

# Requirements
	- The lamp shall provide a control mechanism that allows the user to turn the light on and off, and adjust its brightness. 
	- The lamp shall operate from a 5V DC input supplied from an AC-to-DC wall adapter. 
	- The lamp shall incorporate a custom PCB designed to fit within and mount to a 3D-printed enclosure.
